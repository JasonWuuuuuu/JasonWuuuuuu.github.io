/01 About Me

I'm Jason Wu (Haiyang Wu), a 2025-entry academic Ph.D. candidate in Transportation Engineering at the School of Traffic & Transportation, Chongqing Jiaotong University, advised by Prof. Xiaoyu Cai in the Air-Ground Cooperative Traffic Control & Technical Equipment Laboratory.

My research centers on Connected & Automated Vehicles (CAV) in mixed human–machine traffic — lane-change interaction mechanisms, trajectory planning, causal-knowledge-guided prediction modeling, and deployable traffic AI spanning algorithm R&D, edge deployment, and server-side integration.

I hold a B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering & Automation from Chongqing College of Mobile Communication and an M.Eng. in Electronic Information (Control Engineering) from Xijing University (advisors: Prof. Lingyun Kong & Prof. Lili Han). Trained in classical control & electronics, I now深耕 transportation — yet never lost my love for classical control theory. During undergrad I served as president of the campus literary society: beyond engineering, words & expression matter just as much.

" Engineering algorithms that survive the field — not just the paper. "

Qijiang District · 52 Traffic-Safety Persuasion Stations Digital Front-End Upgrade

2025 — 2026

Role: Algorithm Lead · Technical Director　|　Partner: Qijiang District Public Security Bureau　|　Featured in Chongqing Daily (2026.05)

Tackling the governance challenge on Qijiang's rural roads — "many points, long lines, wide areas" (~5,253 km of roadways; 130k+ registered two-/three-wheel motorcycles; rampant helmet-less / overloading / illegal-loading violations). Completed the smart front-end upgrade of 52 persuasion stations, covering the full pipeline from on-site construction to algorithm deployment:

on-site specalgorithm R&Dedge deploymentdevice selectionserver integration
  • Six-category violation recognition — helmet-less, overloading, illegal-loading, wrong-way, crossing-line, speeding — 24/7 automated detection & capture.
  • End-edge-cloud closed loop — front-end recognition → district public-security smart-traffic management center → on-site speaker / LED correction → barrier auto-release after hazard cleared.
  • Five-in-one function — violation recognition, smart persuasion, info collection, safety promotion, collision warning.
  • Real-world results — Wenlong Subdistrict alone captured 600+ helmet-less / overloading violations, processed 24 overloading cases, conducted 500+ educational sessions, tiered management of 24 individuals; pilot areas saw gradual decline in violations.

Knowledge-Infused Prediction of Post-Lane-Change Vehicle Interaction Strength

2025 — Now

Longitudinal research · IEEE TVT under review (2nd round)

Studying the chain effect of lane-change vehicles on following traffic; proposes a vehicle-queue interaction prediction model. Uses Interaction Influence Strength (IIS) hierarchical clustering to identify explainable interaction patterns, observes transition features during lane changes, and proves that introducing interaction-pattern priors significantly improves multi-scenario prediction accuracy — supporting multi-vehicle cooperative decision-making for autonomous driving.

Staged Detection Framework for the "Ghost-Pedestrian" Problem in Autonomous Driving

2025 — Now

Active research topic

Addressing the occlusion-induced "ghost-pedestrian" challenge with a staged processing framework that boosts detection accuracy. Designs a posture-estimation matrix for object tracking, proposes a probe-style data-augmentation method and motion-trend visualization, and performs pedestrian-crossing-intent classification & prediction on the JAAD dataset.
